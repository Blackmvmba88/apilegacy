# Acceptable Use Policy — BlackMamba Template

BlackMamba software must not be used to:

- violate applicable law;
- compromise accounts, devices, systems, or services without authorization;
- distribute malware or malicious payloads;
- steal credentials or conduct unauthorized surveillance;
- circumvent security, access-control, anti-cheat, or platform-integrity mechanisms;
- harass, defraud, impersonate, or unlawfully discriminate against others;
- intentionally disrupt networks or services;
- automate actions prohibited by a connected third-party platform;
- conceal prohibited activity behind BlackMamba infrastructure.

## Human-input bridge rule

For projects that translate physical or human input into application or game controls, the default integrity rule is:

**one human action -> one control action**

The baseline excludes autonomous aiming, recoil compensation, botting, prohibited macro chains, unauthorized memory inspection, and anti-cheat circumvention.

## Enforcement

Projects may reject requests, revoke credentials, disable sessions, or impose technical limits when reasonably necessary to protect users, infrastructure, third parties, or service integrity.

## Project-specific extension

Each downstream project may add stricter restrictions when its risk model, platform integration, or regulatory environment requires them.
