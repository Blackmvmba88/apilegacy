## Trademark rule

Third-party product names and marks are used only for accurate identification, compatibility description, or attribution unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

Compatibility does not imply affiliation, sponsorship, certification, approval, or endorsement.

## Dependency rule

A downstream project must not assume that a dependency is commercially usable merely because its source code is publicly available. Its actual license and obligations must be reviewed.

## Platform-rule inheritance

When BlackMamba software connects to or operates on top of a third-party platform, the integration must respect applicable platform terms, developer policies, security controls, and integrity rules.

## Notices and attribution

Required copyright notices, license texts, attribution statements, and source-offer obligations must be preserved where the applicable third-party license requires them.
